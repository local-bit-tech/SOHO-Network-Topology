# VLAN's and STP

First, We will configure VLAN's on Switch 1 and Switch 2.
VLAN's are important to seperate your broadcast domains to make your network topology safer.

First, we will go into privelleged exec mode then into global configuration mode to configure the switches.

To configure the VLAN's and their names we run these commands:
```text
SW1(config)# vlan 10
````
